Privacy

Short version: your practice data never leaves your device.

What is stored

Your answers, scores, streak, skill estimates and settings are saved in your browser's local storage (or, in the Android/iOS app, in the app's private storage). They are not sent to any server. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app deletes them. You can export or delete them at any time from More → Your data.

Accounts

There are none. The app works without signing in.

Analytics

The website may count page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that sets no cookies and collects no personal data. Counting is skipped if your browser sends "Do Not Track". The native apps do not count anything.

Third parties

Links to sources (e.g. Glassdoor, Wikipedia, SEC filings) open those sites, which have their own policies. The app does not embed advertising or tracking from anyone else.
